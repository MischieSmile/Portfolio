About

I’m a software developer who has worked on all kinds of projects. I mainly develop backend systems, but I enjoy bringing a whole application together, from the database to the interface. I’m always keen to learn a new technology and turn an idea into an app or a website.

My curiosity started when I was five, with an old computer running Windows XP. I used to explore the operating system’s files, trying to understand what everything did. As I grew up, that curiosity never left. I still look at software and wonder how it works, then find myself reading about it or trying to build something of my own.

I’ve worked in small and large teams, taken on freelance work, and built my own projects through Velkina Studio. Those experiences have taught me how to work with different people, understand what they need, and keep learning as a project grows. My work often involves APIs, databases, automation, and connecting different services.

I also love making small programs and game mods that make everyday life a little easier for me. Sometimes a project starts with something I wish existed, or a task I’m tired of doing by hand. Working out a solution and then using it myself is still one of my favourite parts of programming.
